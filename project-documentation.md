# LungVisionAI - Project Documentation

## 1. Problem Statement

Lung diseases such as Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Lung Cancer, COPD and Fibrosis can cause serious health complications.

Existing Deep Learning systems often focus on a limited number of diseases and may provide predictions without clearly explaining why a particular disease was detected.

LungVisionAI aims to develop a multi-model Deep Learning framework for detecting multiple lung diseases from chest X-ray and suitable MRI images, while providing explainable predictions and disease-specific general health guidance.

---

## 2. Research Need

- Existing models may focus on a limited number of diseases.
- Many Deep Learning models provide predictions with limited interpretability.
- Complex models can be difficult to understand.
- Detection and explainability are often addressed separately.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Develop a multi-disease lung disease detection system.
- Compare different Deep Learning architectures.
- Apply Explainable AI to visualize important image regions.
- Support suitable chest X-ray and MRI images where applicable.
- Provide disease-specific general precautions and health suggestions.
- Evaluate the models using standard performance metrics.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

LungVisionAI combines:

1. Medical image preprocessing
2. Multi-model Deep Learning
3. Lung disease classification
4. Explainable AI
5. Disease-specific health guidance

## 6. Research Direction

The project research focuses on:

- Multi-disease lung classification
- Transfer Learning
- Convolutional Neural Networks
- Transformer-based architectures
- Explainable AI
- Medical image analysis

---

## 7. Evaluation

The models will be evaluated using:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-score
- AUC

Additional evaluation will be considered for the quality and usefulness of model explanations.

---

## 8. Project Scope

LungVisionAI is intended as a research and preliminary screening system.

It is not intended to replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment.
